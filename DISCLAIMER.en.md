# Disclaimer and Terms of Use

**Please read this page before using the project.** Installing or using it means
you have read and accepted the terms below.

---

## 1. Not for medical use

This project is a **tool for everyday assistance and companionship. It is not a
medical device**, and it does not constitute professional medical advice, a
dementia diagnosis, or treatment of any kind.

For anything to do with actual care, medication or a change in condition,
**follow the guidance of the doctors and nurses looking after the person.**

Features such as "what your elder has been saying" report raw counts and times
only. They **make no health assessment and no judgement about the progression of
any condition.** If you see something that concerns you, take it to a doctor —
do not let it stand in for a consultation.

## 2. It is not an emergency response system

The system can notify family when it hears words suggesting distress. This is a
**best-effort reminder — not an emergency call, a medical alarm, or a monitoring
service.**

It will fail in the following situations, all of which are common:

- The computer is switched off, has crashed, has lost its connection, or the
  notification service (e.g. Telegram) is unavailable
- The person says nothing, cannot speak, or says something that matches no keyword
- Speech recognition mishears, or the system is busy with something else

**In an emergency, call your local emergency number** (999 in the UK, 911 in the
US, 119 in Taiwan).
Please do not reduce visits or existing care arrangements because this is installed.

## 3. Privacy and security: yours to control, and yours to maintain

The system is designed to run **entirely on your own machine**. Recordings,
conversation history, uploaded photos and consent records all stay on your device.

**Neither this project nor its author operates, holds, or maintains any server
that collects user data.** The author cannot access your data and cannot help
recover it.

In return, **the security of your own machine is your responsibility**: operating
system updates, anti-virus, physical access, whether you expose it to the
internet, and safekeeping of the family console password. If you choose to make
the system reachable from outside your home network, make sure access control is
configured first.

## 4. Lawful use of voice cloning

Before setting up or cloning a voice, **you must obtain the informed consent of
the person whose voice it is.**

Using this system or its generated audio for **fraud, impersonation, harassment,
or anything unlawful where you live is strictly prohibited.**

The spoken-consent gate and the AI watermark built into this project exist to
make legitimate use easy and to let generated audio be verified afterwards as
synthetic. **They are design aids, not legal guarantees, and they cannot prevent
deliberate misuse** — a watermark can be lost through re-encoding, and both
features can be switched off by whoever runs the software.

Any legal dispute or liability arising from misuse **rests with the user**.
