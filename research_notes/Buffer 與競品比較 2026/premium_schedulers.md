# Premium / mid-market schedulers as Buffer competitors (2026): Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Agorapulse, Later

Research date: 2026-10-29. **Method caveat:** this session could not reach the vendors' own domains. hootsuite.com, sproutsocial.com, agorapulse.com and later.com all failed DNS because they are outside the session's allowed network domains. Every finding below comes from web-search results: some quote vendor help-centre or blog pages, but most come from third-party blogs, and many of those are competitor or affiliate sites with a commercial bias. To confirm prices, allow those domains in the session's network settings and check hootsuite.com/plans, sproutsocial.com/pricing, agorapulse.com/pricing and later.com/pricing directly. User constraints used for the fit verdicts: £0–30 a month, a single user, Threads + Instagram Professional + YouTube Shorts automated through an API or MCP, Facebook personal profile and Xiaohongshu handled manually.

## Q1. Pricing: free plan or trial, cheapest solo tier, per-seat vs per-channel, contract terms

### Takeaway
Hootsuite (about $99/user/mo), Sprout Social ($79–$199/seat/mo) and Agorapulse ($79/user/mo) are all 3–7x over the £30 ceiling, and none of them now has a free plan. Only **Later Starter** fits the budget, at $18.75/mo billed yearly or $25/mo billed monthly, roughly £15–20. Later has no public API, though, which breaks the agent-to-scheduler requirement (see Q3).

### Cited Findings
**Hootsuite**
- Standard is $99/user/mo on annual billing and includes 10 social accounts. Professional is $199 and Advanced is $399; Enterprise is a custom quote. — [lilachbullock.com](https://www.lilachbullock.com/hootsuite-pricing/); [upload-post.com](https://www.upload-post.com/hootsuite-pricing/); [costbench.com](https://costbench.com/software/social-media-management/hootsuite/); [socialpilot.co](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/hootsuite-pricing)
- There is no free plan, only a 14-day free trial of the paid plans. — [socialpilot.co](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/hootsuite-pricing); [postiv.ai](https://postiv.ai/blog/hootsuite-pricing)
- **Conflict on billing:** one source says Hootsuite shows only annual billing. Others say monthly billing is available on Standard, Professional and Advanced at a higher per-user rate. — [socialpilot.co](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/hootsuite-pricing) vs [lilachbullock.com](https://www.lilachbullock.com/hootsuite-pricing/)
- **Conflicts on tiers:** one source says Professional no longer exists, and another lists Standard at $199 (an outlier). One source gives Standard 5 accounts; most say 10. — [createsocial.ai](https://createsocial.ai/blog/hootsuite-pricing-breakdown); [tools8020.com](https://tools8020.com/tools/hootsuite/pricing/); [turrboo.com](https://turrboo.com/blog/hootsuite-pricing)
- **GBP:** a single UK review quotes Standard at £89/user/mo (£1,068 a year) and Professional at £169. It is unverified against Hootsuite's UK page. EUR pricing is reported as €99 and €199. — [ukbusinesssoftware.co.uk](https://ukbusinesssoftware.co.uk/hootsuite-review-uk-2026-2/); [smartkeys.org](https://smartkeys.org/hootsuite-review/)
- Hootsuite relaunched as "Social OS" on 24 June 2026. The product is now separate apps: Perch (publishing), Nest (inbox), Lumen (listening, built on Talkwalker) and Parliament (employee advocacy), plus an AI agent called "Wisdom". The apps unlock by tier. — [smartkeys.org](https://smartkeys.org/hootsuite-review/); [reputation-insider.com](https://www.reputation-insider.com/hootsuite-review/); [blog.hootsuite.com](https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-ai-tools/)

**Sprout Social**
- Pricing is per seat. A newer **Essentials** tier is $79/seat/mo billed annually or $99 billed monthly. Standard is $199/seat/mo billed annually (about $249 monthly). Advanced is $399/seat/mo, and Enterprise is a custom quote. — [zapro.ai](https://zapro.ai/vendor/sprout-social-pricing/); [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/sprout-social-pricing); [octolens.com](https://octolens.com/blog/sprout-social-pricing); [socialcrawl.dev](https://www.socialcrawl.dev/blog/sprout-social-pricing)
- Listening and Premium Analytics are paid add-ons with unpublished prices. — [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/sprout-social-pricing)
- **Staleness flag:** the copy of sproutsocial.com/pricing seen in search results was about 646 days old. Third-party pages were fresher. — [sproutsocial.com/pricing](https://sproutsocial.com/pricing/)
- No free plan was found. I found no 2026 source for the trial length (see Gaps).

**Agorapulse**
- Pricing is per user. Standard is $79/user/mo billed annually or $99 billed monthly. Professional is $119 and Advanced is $149 (annual). Every extra user costs the same as the first, and the 10 profile slots are shared across users. — [postplanify.com](https://postplanify.com/agorapulse-pricing); [socialpilot.co](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/agorapulse-pricing); [napoleoncat.com](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/agorapulse-pricing/)
- **Free plan retired.** The most recent source says it was retired on 6 July 2026, while Agorapulse's own pricing FAQ still mentioned it. Some aggregators still list "Free: 1 user, 3 accounts", which looks stale. — [napoleoncat.com](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/agorapulse-pricing/); [socialrails.com](https://socialrails.com/blog/agorapulse-pricing); [aisotools.com](https://aisotools.com/pricing/agorapulse)
- There is a 30-day free trial with no credit card. A free tier of **Archie**, Agorapulse's AI content tool, allows publishing only through Archie. — [napoleoncat.com](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/agorapulse-pricing/)

**Later**
- Later prices by "social set" (one profile per supported network). — [later.com/pricing via archive.com](https://archive.com/blog/later-pricing); [socialpilot.co](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/later-pricing)
  - Starter: $18.75/mo billed yearly or $25/mo billed monthly. It covers 1 social set and 1 user, with 30 posts per profile per month, 5 AI credits per month and about 3 months of analytics.
  - Growth: $37.50/mo billed yearly, about $50 monthly.
  - Scale: $82.50/mo billed yearly, about $110 monthly.
- A second user forces an upgrade to Growth. Extra social sets, users and AI credits are each paid add-ons. — [socialpilot.co](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/later-pricing); [socialk.it](https://socialk.it/en/pricing/later)
- **Free plan is contested.** Most sources say Later discontinued it (one says in 2024) and now offers a 14-day trial on all tiers. Blotato, a competitor, says a hidden free plan still exists with 12 posts per profile per month, 0 AI credits and a 2-week calendar. — [lilachbullock.com](https://www.lilachbullock.com/later-pricing/); [posteverywhere.ai](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/later-alternatives); [blotato.com](https://www.blotato.com/blog/later-pricing)

### Inferences
- Approximate GBP conversions use roughly £0.75–0.80 per $1. That rate is my assumption, not sourced, and UK VAT may be added on top.
  - Hootsuite Standard is about £75–89/mo.
  - Sprout Essentials is about £60–79/mo; Standard is about £150–199/mo.
  - Agorapulse Standard is about £60–79/mo.
  - Later Starter is about £14–20/mo, the only one under £30.
- All four lock the cheapest price behind annual prepayment. That is risky for a side-project user whose needs may change.

### Gaps
- No vendor pricing page could be fetched, so official UK GBP prices for all four are unverified.
- Sprout's current trial length and the exact feature set of Essentials were not confirmed.
- Hootsuite's monthly-billing availability and price are contested.

## Q2. Network coverage for the user's platforms (and others)

### Takeaway
All four cover Instagram and YouTube in some form. Threads is covered by Hootsuite, Sprout and Later, and Agorapulse lists it in its API but has little Threads-specific documentation. Bluesky is clear for Sprout and Agorapulse, contested for Hootsuite, and absent for Later. None of the four supports Xiaohongshu or other Chinese platforms. None can post to Facebook Groups, because Meta removed the Groups API in 2024.

### Cited Findings
- **Hootsuite (official FAQ):** supports Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, WhatsApp and Pinterest. — [hootsuite.com/faq](https://www.hootsuite.com/faq)
  - Its older integrations page says all plans can publish, schedule and draft for Threads, and that Bluesky posts can be scheduled. That page is about 1,372 days old. — [hootsuite.com/platform/integrations](https://www.hootsuite.com/platform/integrations)
  - **Conflict on Bluesky:** one 2026 review says Hootsuite "notably lacks support for Bluesky, Mastodon, and Google Business Profile". Reddit is not mentioned anywhere, and YouTube Shorts are not mentioned specifically. — [modelranked.com](https://modelranked.com/social-media-management/hootsuite-review/) and other results in that search
- **Sprout Social (help centre):** lists Threads, Bluesky, TikTok and YouTube as publishing networks, plus X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest. — [support.sproutsocial.com](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005707863-What-social-profiles-can-I-connect-to-Sprout); [support.sproutsocial.com (post types)](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/15632585403021-What-types-of-posts-can-I-publish-using-Sprout-Social)
  - YouTube video and Shorts are supported, but YouTube image posts are not. TikTok Stories are not supported. Threads polls must be made natively. — [support.sproutsocial.com](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003659326-Media-Upload-Types-and-Size-Limits)
  - The mobile apps cannot publish to Snapchat, Reddit or Bluesky, so Reddit appears to be supported on desktop. — same help-centre results
  - Instagram auto-publish is claimed only by a review site. — [bestsocialmediascheduler.com](https://bestsocialmediascheduler.com/reviews/sprout-social)
- **Agorapulse:**
  - Instagram: Reels, Stories and Carousels publish directly, and Reels can use only original audio through the API. — [support.agorapulse.com](https://support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/10520824-publishing-by-social-network-explained)
  - TikTok: images and video only.
  - YouTube: Shorts are auto-detected (vertical and under 3 minutes, with no override). Custom Shorts thumbnails, community posts and unlisted videos are not supported. — [support.agorapulse.com API limits](https://support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/8712660-api-limitations-per-network)
  - Bluesky is in the composer, and Bluesky video cannot have subtitle files. — [intercom.help/agorapulse](https://intercom.help/agorapulse/en/articles/8769280-publishing-composer-explained)
  - Threads and Bluesky appear as channel types in its profile API. — [apis.io](https://apis.io/apis/agorapulse/agorapulse-profile-api/)
  - Pinterest appears in marketing copy only. — [agorapulse.com](https://agorapulse.com/features/social-media-publishing/)
- **Later (help centre):** supports Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, TikTok, LinkedIn, Threads, YouTube **Shorts only** (no long-form) and Snapchat. Bluesky is not listed. — [help.later.com](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060842914-Supported-Social-Platforms-Post-Types)
- **Facebook Groups:** Meta retired the Groups API on 22 April 2024, removing the `publish_to_groups` permission. Schedulers now offer only reminder-based "manual" group posting, while Pages are unaffected. — [techpoint.africa](https://techpoint.africa/2024/02/06/meta-facebook-disrupts-scheduling); [help.zoho.com](https://help.zoho.com/portal/zh/community/topic/discontinuing-facebook-groups-due-to-api-deprecation?page=10); [cloudcampaign.com](https://www.cloudcampaign.com/answers/schedule-facebook-group-posts)
- **Chinese platforms:** no source confirms Xiaohongshu, WeChat or Weibo scheduling in any of the four. Guides say such platforms need specialised tools. — [hashmeta.com](https://hashmeta.com/blog/top-20-social-media-management-tools-the-complete-guide-for-marketing-teams)

### Inferences
- Facebook personal profiles are almost certainly not publishable by any of the four. Meta has long blocked third-party posting to personal profiles. This comes from background knowledge and no 2026 source was retrieved, so treat it as unverified. It matches the user's plan to post to Facebook and Xiaohongshu manually.
- For the user's core trio (Threads, Instagram, YouTube Shorts), Sprout, Hootsuite and Later all appear to cover the networks. The deciding factors are price and API, not networks.

### Gaps
- Whether Hootsuite supports Bluesky and YouTube Shorts is unconfirmed.
- Whether Agorapulse auto-publishes to Threads is unconfirmed.
- Reddit support is unclear for Hootsuite, Agorapulse and Later.
- No vendor page on personal-profile publishing was retrieved.

## Q3. API, MCP/Claude and Zapier/Make: can a Claude cloud agent push drafts in?

### Takeaway
Hootsuite and Agorapulse both now have **official remote MCP connectors usable from Claude**. They are draft-oriented and require paid plans of about $79–99 or more per month. Sprout's official MCP is ChatGPT-only and limited to TikTok insights, and its API is reportedly an Advanced-tier ($399/seat) feature. Later has no public API and no MCP. So the only budget-fitting tool (Later) is the one an agent cannot drive.

### Cited Findings
- **Hootsuite MCP:** official servers exist for Perch, Nest, Lumen and Parliament and work with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot. — [hootsuite.com/integrations/mcp](https://www.hootsuite.com/integrations/mcp); [hootsuite.com/docs](https://www.hootsuite.com/docs)
  - Perch is at `https://mcp.hootsuite.com/perch` and is added as a Claude custom connector with OAuth sign-in. Sample prompts save drafts, for example an unpublished LinkedIn draft. — [blog.hootsuite.com](https://blog.hootsuite.com/post-on-social-media-from-claude/)
  - It launched with the 24 June 2026 relaunch and works in claude.ai. — [kompozy.io](https://kompozy.io/reviews/hootsuite-claude-connector)
  - **Plan gating is undocumented.** One source says Hootsuite does not say which plans include MCP, and parts of Parliament and Wisdom were "coming soon" at launch. — [dev.to](https://dev.to/olver_thorarinsson_bcf885/which-social-media-schedulers-support-mcp-every-tool-compared-2026-e87); [smartkeys.org](https://smartkeys.org/hootsuite-review/)
  - **Conflict:** Socialync, a competitor, says Hootsuite has no native MCP server. This is contradicted by Hootsuite's own pages. — [socialync.io](https://www.socialync.io/mcp-server/hootsuite)
  - Hootsuite also has a developer API and Zapier/Make connections. — [socialync.io](https://www.socialync.io/mcp-server/hootsuite)
- **Sprout Social:**
  - **API tier:** the Sprout API is listed under Advanced ($399/seat) and Enterprise, not Standard. — [socialcrawl.dev](https://www.socialcrawl.dev/blog/sprout-social-pricing); [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/sprout-social-pricing)
  - **Conflict:** one guide claims Standard includes the API. Most sources treat that as an error. — [postzen.dev](https://www.postzen.dev/blog/sprout-social-api)
  - **API limits:** reportedly 60 requests per minute and 250,000 per month. It reads data and creates drafts in your own account; it is not for publishing on behalf of end users. Drafts cover 9 networks, with no Bluesky. — [postzen.dev](https://www.postzen.dev/blog/sprout-social-api); [twitterapi.io](https://twitterapi.io/blog/sprout-social-api)
  - **Official MCP:** Sprout supports ChatGPT only, has not tested Claude, and the scope is TikTok insights and metrics. — [support.sproutsocial.com](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/41085761844365-ChatGPT-Integration-Overview-and-Setup)
  - **Unofficial community MCP:** a 28-tool server covers drafts, scheduling and media upload. — [github.com/oliverames/sprout-mcp-server](https://github.com/oliverames/sprout-mcp-server); [github.com/EbonyLouis/sprout-social-mcp](https://github.com/EbonyLouis/sprout-social-mcp)
- **Agorapulse:**
  - **Official MCP connector:** works with Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot through hosted OAuth at `https://api.openmcp.agorapulse.com/mcp`. It is in **beta**, and **Free and Legacy plans are excluded**. — [support.agorapulse.com](https://support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/12960496-model-context-protocool-mcp-integration-explained)
  - It creates drafts only, and you schedule or publish them inside Agorapulse. — [yavio.ai](https://yavio.ai/directory/agorapulse)
  - **Public API:** read-only Analytics API on the Custom enterprise plan only, capped at 500 requests per 30 minutes. This comes from Supergood, a vendor with a competing product. — [supergood.ai](https://supergood.ai/api-report-card/agorapulse)
  - An older npm MCP package (`@agorapulse/mcp`, about 504 days old) wraps the API with an API key. — [npmjs.com](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@agorapulse/mcp)
- **Later:**
  - **MCP:** a 2026 comparison found none. — [dev.to](https://dev.to/olver_thorarinsson_bcf885/which-social-media-schedulers-support-mcp-every-tool-compared-2026-e87)
  - **Public API:** "Not available" per vendor comparisons. — [info.bundle.social](https://info.bundle.social/comparison/later); [socialpostflow.com](https://www.socialpostflow.com/social-media-scheduler-comparison/)
  - **Zapier:** Zapier's listing said Later is "not yet available" (page about 1,569 days old, may be stale). — [zapier.com](https://zapier.com/apps/later-com/integrations)
  - Blotato's headline says "No Publishing API". — [blotato.com](https://www.blotato.com/blog/later-pricing)

### Inferences
- Hootsuite's Perch MCP and Agorapulse's MCP fit the user's workflow well: the agent writes a draft into the scheduler and the human approves in the app. The price of about $79–99/mo is the blocker, not the capability.
- The draft-only MCP design is actually a good match for the "I only review and approve" model.
- A Claude cloud agent needs a remote, OAuth or HTTP-reachable MCP. Hootsuite's and Agorapulse's are hosted. The Sprout community servers are self-hosted and need a Sprout API plan on top.

### Gaps
- Which Hootsuite tier includes the MCP is unconfirmed.
- Whether Agorapulse's MCP can schedule rather than only draft in late 2026 is unconfirmed.
- Hootsuite developer-programme restrictions (app review, plan requirement) were not retrieved.
- No Make (Integromat) details were found for any of the four.

## Q4. Workflow features: drafts and approvals, inbox, analytics, AI

### Takeaway
All four have drafts, analytics and AI writing. Formal approval workflows sit on mid or upper tiers, but a solo user doesn't need them: the MCP draft queue plus their own manual approval already gives "review then publish".

### Cited Findings
- **Hootsuite:**
  - Review and approval workflows are on Advanced, the top self-serve tier. — [smartkeys.org](https://smartkeys.org/hootsuite-review/)
  - The inbox is Nest. The AI agent is Wisdom, which spans the plans. — [blog.hootsuite.com](https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-ai-tools/); [reputation-insider.com](https://www.reputation-insider.com/hootsuite-review/)
  - Whether the OwlyWriter brand continues after the Social OS relaunch was not confirmed.
- **Sprout Social:**
  - Standard adds automation and Smart Inbox sentiment. Premium Analytics, needed for some X metrics, and Listening are paid add-ons. — [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/sprout-social-pricing); [postzen.dev](https://www.postzen.dev/blog/sprout-social-api)
- **Agorapulse:**
  - Archie is its AI content tool, with a free tier that publishes only through Archie. — [napoleoncat.com](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/agorapulse-pricing/)
- **Later:**
  - Starter includes 5 AI credits per month and about 3 months of analytics history. Extra AI credits are paid add-ons. — [socialpilot.co](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/later-pricing); [socialk.it](https://socialk.it/en/pricing/later)

### Inferences
- With Claude writing the posts, the schedulers' own AI writers (Wisdom, OwlyWriter, Archie, Later AI credits) add little value for this user.

### Gaps
- Exact per-tier inbox and analytics limits were not retrieved because vendor pages were blocked.
- Sprout AI feature names and their tier gating were not retrieved.

## Q5. Reviews, pitfalls 2025–2026, UK availability, and overall fit

### Takeaway
2026 has seen consolidation upmarket:
- Agorapulse retired its free plan in July 2026.
- Later's free plan is gone or hidden, and it now charges per social set with add-ons.
- Hootsuite relaunched in June 2026 with a multi-app structure, and some features were still "coming soon".
- Sprout keeps its API behind a $399 seat.

All four sell to UK customers; I found no restriction. **Verdict for this user:**
- None of the three API/MCP-capable tools fits £0–30.
- Later Starter fits the budget but can't be driven by an agent.

So all four are a poor match. The user should look at budget API-first schedulers instead, which other researchers are covering.

### Cited Findings
- Agorapulse's free plan was retired on 6 July 2026. Its per-user pricing means each added seat costs the full price. — [napoleoncat.com](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/agorapulse-pricing/); [socialpilot.co](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/agorapulse-pricing)
- On Later, extra sets, users and AI credits are each paid add-ons. G2 and Capterra reviewers report costs rising as accounts and teammates are added. A "social-set pricing trap" is described by a competitor. — [socialk.it](https://socialk.it/en/pricing/later); [withsynapse.app](https://withsynapse.app/blog/later-alternative)
- Some Hootsuite Social OS capabilities (parts of Parliament and Wisdom) were "coming soon" at launch. — [smartkeys.org](https://smartkeys.org/hootsuite-review/)
- Hootsuite reviews (G2) for 2026 are available, but their contents were not retrieved in detail. — [g2.com](https://www.g2.com/products/hootsuite/reviews)
- A UK-specific Hootsuite review (prices in £) exists, which suggests UK sales and GBP billing. — [ukbusinesssoftware.co.uk](https://ukbusinesssoftware.co.uk/hootsuite-review-uk-2026-2/)
- Sprout's Advanced API tier costs about $23,940 a year for 5 seats, versus $11,940 on Standard. This shows how steep the price is for API access. — [socialcrawl.dev](https://www.socialcrawl.dev/blog/sprout-social-pricing)

### Inferences
Fit summary for £0–30/mo, solo, agent pushes drafts:

| Tool | Cheapest solo (≈£/mo) | Free plan? | Threads / IG / YT Shorts | Agent route (API/MCP) | Fits? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hootsuite | ~£75–89 (Standard, annual) | No (14-day trial) | Yes / Yes / YouTube yes, Shorts unconfirmed | Official MCP (Perch, drafts); tier unclear | No: price |
| Sprout Social | ~£60–79 (Essentials) / ~£150–199 (Standard) | No | Yes / Yes / Yes | API only on Advanced ($399); official MCP ChatGPT-only | No: price and API tier |
| Agorapulse | ~£60–79 (Standard) | Retired July 2026 (30-day trial) | Listed / Yes / Yes | Official MCP (beta, drafts, paid plans only) | No: price |
| Later | ~£14–20 (Starter) | Disputed; 14-day trial | Yes / Yes / Yes (Shorts only) | No public API, no MCP, Zapier doubtful | Price fits, but no agent route |

- A trial-hopping approach (Agorapulse 30-day, Hootsuite 14-day) could be used to evaluate the MCP draft flow. That isn't a sustainable £0 solution.
- Lock-in risk: annual prepayment is needed for the headline prices on all four, and per-seat pricing punishes later growth.

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads were retrieved for 2025–2026 user sentiment.
- No specific 2025–2026 Hootsuite or Sprout price-hike dates were confirmed.
- UK availability is inferred from GBP and EUR price mentions, not from vendor statements.
- No vendor primary pages could be fetched in this session (network allowlist).
